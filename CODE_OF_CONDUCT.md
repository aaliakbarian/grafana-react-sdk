# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in `grafana-react-sdk` a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

Project participants are expected to:

- communicate with empathy, patience, and respect;
- give and receive constructive technical feedback;
- focus criticism on ideas and outcomes, not people;
- acknowledge mistakes and make a good-faith effort to repair harm;
- respect privacy, boundaries, and differing viewpoints; and
- prioritize the health of the community and project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or threats;
- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention;
- insults, trolling, personal or political attacks, and sustained disruption;
- publishing another person's private information without permission; and
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and whenever someone represents the project in public, including repositories, issue trackers, review discussions, community channels, and events.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers. Use the repository host's private reporting or moderation channel when available; do not include sensitive details in a public issue. Dedicated contact information will be published as the community infrastructure is established.

Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect the reporter's privacy as far as reasonably possible, and respond in proportion to the circumstances. Possible actions include a private warning, removal of content, temporary restrictions, or permanent removal from project spaces.

Maintainers who have a conflict of interest must not handle the report. Retaliation against anyone who raises a good-faith concern is prohibited.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant and common open-source community standards. It is intentionally concise and may evolve as the project's community and reporting channels mature.
